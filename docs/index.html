How it works

  • Data: daily ERA5 reanalysis from Open-Meteo since 1990 for Urbana plus four upstream points (Peoria, Springfield, Davenport and St. Louis), because weather here mostly arrives from the west.
  • Features: the last week of temperature, dew point, pressure and its trend, clouds, wind and rain, along with the season.
  • Models: regularized linear models and gradient-boosted trees, one per variable and lead time. The one with the lower test error is used.
  • Honest testing: models train on days before 2021 and are scored only on later days they never saw.
